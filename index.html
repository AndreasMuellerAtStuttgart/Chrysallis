Chrysalis is the web version of a poetry album, consisting of poems I wrote on deep underground poetry (which sadly doesn't exist anymore) over the span of multiple years. Those years were a challenging time for me, because I re-entered the workforce after several years of recovering from a traumatic experience.

All the work done in rehabilitation to make it back has payed of. I have now, as I write this, been a professional software developer for over 6 years.

Chrysalis is the story of how I made it back, and it's also the story of my first few years back working. Looking back on it, the poems deal a lot with finding a safe haven within myself, which isn't dependend on external factors.

I am even further along in this journey than the last poem indicates. Nowadays, my personal faith is a mixture of Buddhism and the Christian faith I was raised with. I trust in the power of the present moment to reveal everything that needs to be revealed so I can follow my path. And my path is doing good deeds, for myself and others.

And I know that if I try my best to follow this path, when I die my immortal soul will be embraced by the father, the force that has guided me all my life, which has whispered in the darkest times: Do not let go of life. There is still so much for you to experience, and so much to do.

In the end this voice was always right. I wish you happy reading, and may the poems and the stories surrounding them bring you joy and inspiration for your own path.
